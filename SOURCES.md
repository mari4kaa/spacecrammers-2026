## Flammability boundary

- Olson, S.L.; Ferkul, P.V.; Bhattacharjee, S.; Miller, F.J.; Fernandez-Pello, C.; Link, S.; T'ien, J.S.; Wichman, I.: Burning and Suppression of Solids-II (BASS-II) Summary Report. NASA/TM-20210011385, NASA Glenn Research Center, October 2021. [NASA NTRS record](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20210011385) · [PSI-25 investigation](https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/data/investigations/PSI-25) · [DOI 10.60555/4qc4-de67](https://doi.org/10.60555/4qc4-de67)
  - Used for: All boundary data. PMMA rods: Figure 6.10 (digitized, every marker checked by eye) and the report's fitted equation, Eq. 6.1 (shown as a dashed line). Cotton-fiberglass (SIBAL) fabric: Table 7.1. Thin PMMA sheet: Table 2.1. Test conditions and caveats: chapters 2, 6 and 7. Read from the copy in PSI investigation PSI-25.
- Hirsch, D., et al.: Issues Related to the Flammability Assessment of Polymers for Hazard Analyses of Oxygen Systems. In Flammability and Sensitivity of Materials in Oxygen-Enriched Atmospheres, 10th Vol., ASTM STP1454, 2003, pp. 21-26.
  - Used for: Only the 15.4% O2 normal-gravity upward flame-spread limit for PMMA (with an extra 4 cm/s forced flow), taken as quoted in the BASS-II report (ref. 23 of chapter 6). The paper itself was not read.
- InfoFlame: digitized points, fitting script and method notes (this project). [Method notes (README)](https://github.com/mari4kaa/spacecrammers-2026/blob/main/data/boundary/README.md) · [Points (CSV)](https://github.com/mari4kaa/spacecrammers-2026/blob/main/data/boundary/bass2_boundary_points.csv) · [Fitting script](https://github.com/mari4kaa/spacecrammers-2026/blob/main/scripts/flammability_boundary.py)
  - Used for: How the points were captured and checked, the refit (same equation form as the report, 3,000 bootstrap resamples, 90% bands, leave-one-out check) and all results.

## Outside PSI: partial gravity and upcoming work

- LUCI (2025): First >25 s simulated lunar-gravity burns (spinning New Shepard). SIBAL and PMMA rod spread rates. [https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20250010653](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20250010653)
- FM2 (planned late 2026): First combustion experiment on the Moon: 4 solid samples in quiescent habitable atmosphere. Will look for the flammability limit at 8.2 psia. [https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20260001966/downloads/Ferkul-Lunar%20and%20Planetary%20Science%20Conf%202026%20v2.pdf](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20260001966/downloads/Ferkul-Lunar%20and%20Planetary%20Science%20Conf%202026%20v2.pdf)
- SoFIE (GEL, MIST): CIR insert for solid fuels at 10-40% O2, 0.2-1.5 atm, 0-30 cm/s. Results not yet in PSI. [https://ntrs.nasa.gov/archive/nasa/casi.ntrs.nasa.gov/20200000361.pdf](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/archive/nasa/casi.ntrs.nasa.gov/20200000361.pdf)
